What is CORE?

CORE is the all-in-one platform that keeps your bookings, payments and clients in order.

Who can use CORE?

Anyone running sessions — personal trainers, coaches, wellness professionals and group instructors.

What do I get?

Professional tools that save time, build credibility and help bring in clients.

Why choose CORE over other platforms?

CORE removes the admin pile-up and helps you show up like a professional from one connected place.

How easy is it to set up?

Sign up, add your schedule and share your link. You can be ready in minutes.
