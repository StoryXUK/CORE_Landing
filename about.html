What is CORE?

One place to run your business

Everything essential.
Nothing in the way.

CORE is an all-in-one business platform for coaches, trainers, instructors and independent fitness and wellbeing professionals.

It gives you the practical tools to present your business, sell your services, organise your schedule, take secure payments and stay connected with your clients.

Each part works together, so a customer can discover you, book, pay and return without creating more administration for you.
